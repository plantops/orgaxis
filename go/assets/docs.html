Readiness boundaries

Only a single synthetic tenant is supported in this binary. No real RBAC, OIDC, legal entitlement calculation, statutory payroll, secure multi-tenant isolation or approval authority validation. HTTP 200 reports retrieval, not legal or employee eligibility clearance.

Payroll Workbench v0.3 (synthetic)

These endpoints use illustrative assumptions, not verified statutory Vietnamese tax, BHXH, or employee payroll rules. All values are seeded synthetic records.
